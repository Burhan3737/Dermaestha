Platform settings

Booking lead time

Minimum time before a slot that patients can still book. Applies to future bookings only — existing confirmed appointments are unaffected.

hours

Default: 1 hour. Can be reduced to a minimum of 0.5 hours (30 minutes).

Applies to future bookings only. Slots within the lead-time window are shown as unavailable to patients at booking time. Existing confirmed appointments are never cancelled by changing this value.

Fallback transaction fee

Used only when the payment gateway does not report a per-transaction fee. When the gateway reports a fee, that figure always wins — this fallback is ignored.

%

Range: 0 – 10 %. Applied to the gross booking amount.

PKR

Range: 0 – 500 PKR. Added on top of the percentage.

This fallback feeds the refund amount, the cancellation estimate, and the dashboard fee breakdown identically. An incorrect value will affect all three displays. Validated bounds: percentage 0–10 %, fixed 0–500 PKR.
